### The Design Concept

The mark is a C-frame — three sides of a structural steel square, open on the right. This simultaneously reads as the letter ```C```(ConTech), a building frame under construction, and a network topology. The five circular nodes at the beam junctions are the key detail: they encode the agent network idea without being literal about it. The top beam is brighter (#74c69d) than the structural beams below (#40916c) — this represents the AI intelligence layer sitting above the physical construction layer. It's a subtle metaphor but one that holds up under scrutiny.
