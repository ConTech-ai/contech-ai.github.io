### The Design Concept

The conceptual direction: ConTech.ai sits at the intersection of two worlds — the hard, structural, load-bearing world of construction, and the intelligent, networked, data-driven world of AI. The logo needs to encode both without being literal about either. No hard hats, no circuit boards, no generic leaf icons.

The idea I'm committing to: a stylized ```C``` formed by an incomplete building frame — think the steel skeleton of a high-rise viewed in cross-section, where the structural grid is the letterform. The negative space inside the "C" suggests the building's interior volume. A subtle node at the corner junction implies the AI agent network. It's architectural, technical, and scalable down to 16×16px for the favicon.

A logo is a compression problem — it has to work at 512×512 and at 16×16, convey two worlds simultaneously (construction + AI), and be instantly recognizable in isolation.

The concept I'm committing to: A stylized ```C``` formed by an incomplete steel building frame — three sides of a square, open on the right, where the beams have node junctions at their corners implying a connected agent network. The top beam gets the green accent (#74c69d) to imply the AI intelligence layer sitting above the structural base. The whole mark reads simultaneously as: a building frame under construction, the letter C (ConTech), and a network topology. It's geometric, scalable, and memorable.
